How it works

Step 1Match

Each ticket's route and stops placed on the GTFS route (name, fuzzy and map matching)

Step 2Count

Board +, alight −, running total along the route = riders on every stretch, every hour

Step 3Scale

App tickets are a sample. ×39 for pink-ticket, pass and cash riders, checked against bus capacity

Step 4Level

Seats · standing · crowded · packed, with seat chance and a confidence label

The Ripple idea: crowding spreads. A full bus leaves people behind, who then crowd the next bus and the parallel routes. The planner counts that as extra waiting; operators can simulate it.
